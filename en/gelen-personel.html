Incoming Erasmus+ Staff Mobility

Erasmus+ Staff Mobility enables academic and administrative staff to teach or undertake training at a partner university or organisation in Europe. The steps for visiting staff interested in an Erasmus+ exchange at Fenerbahçe University (FBU) are outlined below.

Incoming Erasmus+ staff at Fenerbahçe University may participate in two types of mobility:

Teaching Mobility

Teaching mobility can be undertaken by:

  • Staff from a higher education institution holding an Erasmus Charter for Higher Education and a valid Inter-Institutional Agreement with FBU;
  • Staff invited from an organisation to deliver teaching activities at FBU.

A minimum of 8 teaching hours is required for a mobility of at least two days. At least two working days are strongly recommended to make a meaningful contribution to the host institution’s teaching programme and international academic life; shorter durations should be exceptional. The maximum duration is eight weeks.

For teaching activities lasting two days or less, weekends or public holidays falling within the stay abroad are generally not funded unless used as travel or working days. The teaching timetable must demonstrate that teaching activities took place during the reported period.

Teaching mobility must take place with one of FBU’s existing partners in the relevant field and be covered by a valid Inter-Institutional Agreement for teaching mobility.

Training Mobility

Staff training is available to academic and administrative staff. It may take place at another higher education institution holding an Erasmus Charter for Higher Education or at an organisation.

Staff training can take three general forms:

  • a. Staff from a higher education institution visit an organisation. The aim is to enable participants to learn through knowledge and skills transfer and acquire practical skills. Activities may include language training, seminars, workshops, courses and conferences. Such activities should not make up the majority of Erasmus+-funded training days; hands-on training, short secondments and similar activities should form the main part of the programme.
  • b. Administrative and other non-teaching staff from a higher education institution visit a partner higher education institution. The aim is to learn from the partner institution’s experience and good practices and develop skills relevant to the participant’s current role. The main activity is a short stay at the partner institution and may include secondments, job shadowing and study visits.
  • c. Academic staff from a higher education institution visit a partner institution for training. Activities may include language training, seminars, workshops, courses and conferences. These activities should not account for the majority of Erasmus+-funded training days; hands-on training, short secondments and similar activities should make up the main part of the programme.

Initial Enquiry and Contact

If you are interested in teaching or training at FBU, the initial steps are:

  1. Identify the department or unit you wish to visit and its contact person.
  2. Send your proposed work plan together with a CV outlining your expertise and how the mobility would benefit both institutions to erasmus@fbu.edu.tr .
  3. Your home university’s Erasmus Office can usually provide guidance and assistance in completing the teaching assignment or work plan.

Documents, Acceptance and Confirmation

After initial contact, the process continues with the Mobility Agreement and its approval:

  1. Send us your signed Mobility Agreement stating your proposed dates. Dates may be adjusted by mutual agreement if necessary.
    Mobility Agreement Template
  2. Once your Mobility Agreement has been approved, the FBU Erasmus Office will issue your Acceptance Letter.
  3. Upon completing your Erasmus+ teaching or training mobility at FBU, the FBU Erasmus Office will issue a Confirmation Letter certifying your activities.

Inter-Institutional Agreements and Important Notes

  • Erasmus+ staff teaching mobility requires a valid Inter-Institutional Agreement between your home university and FBU.
  • If no agreement exists but the relevant FBU unit can host you on mutually suitable dates, the unit must work with its Erasmus+ Coordinator to arrange an Inter-Institutional Agreement.
  • The Inter-Institutional Agreement must be signed by both institutions before the mobility begins.
