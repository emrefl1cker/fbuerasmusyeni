ECTS Grade Conversion Table

The grades of students returning from an Erasmus+ mobility are converted in accordance with the ECTS grade conversion table . Previous decisions made before mobility and any course changes during mobility are taken into account. The receiving institution’s Transcript of Records and earlier Faculty Board decisions concerning the same institution are also considered when converting grades.
