Erasmus Student Charter

What Is the Erasmus Student Charter?

The Erasmus Student Charter explains the rights and responsibilities of students participating in Erasmus+ mobility for studies or traineeships.

It covers guidance, grant and learning agreements, and preparation before mobility; equal treatment, support services and student responsibilities during mobility; and academic recognition, documentation and participant reporting after mobility. It also explains where students can seek assistance if they encounter problems.
