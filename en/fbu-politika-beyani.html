Erasmus Policy Statement

Our Internationalisation Priorities

The Erasmus Policy Statement sets out Fenerbahçe University’s internationalisation approach and priorities for the 2021–2027 Erasmus+ programme period. The statement identifies the following objectives:

  • Mobility and cooperation: Develop student and staff mobility, establish new international academic partnerships, and support joint education and research projects.
  • Institutional capacity and quality: Strengthen the planning, coordination and monitoring of internationalisation activities, while promoting transparent mobility procedures and academic recognition.
  • Access and inclusion: Promote equal opportunities and improve access to education and mobility opportunities, particularly for participants with fewer opportunities.
  • Multilingualism and intercultural learning: Enhance foreign-language education and Turkish-language learning opportunities for international students, and encourage exchange between participants from different cultures.
  • Digital and environmentally responsible processes: Expand the use of digital tools in mobility management, reduce paper consumption, and encourage environmentally friendly practices.

The Erasmus Policy Statement below provides further details on these objectives and the proposed activities.
