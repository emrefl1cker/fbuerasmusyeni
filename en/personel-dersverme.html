Eligibility and Overview

Applicable period: 2026 KA131 / 2026–2027This guide is based on the Turkish first-call document dated 28 September 2026. Rules may change in later calls; the relevant official call and subsequent amendments take precedence.

Staff Mobility for Teaching allows staff with teaching responsibilities at Fenerbahçe University to deliver teaching at an ECHE-holding partner higher education institution with a valid Erasmus+ inter-institutional agreement.

Eligibility

  • Staff employed full- or part-time and actively working at the University, with teaching duties or a teaching assignment.
  • A permanent academic appointment is not required; staff employed under a university contract may apply.

Duration and minimum teaching hours

  • At least two consecutive days and at most two months, excluding travel days.
  • At least eight teaching hours for up to one week; the minimum increases proportionally for longer stays (e.g. 16 hours for two weeks).
  • When combined with training, a mobility of one week or less requires at least four teaching hours; the combined activity is classified as teaching.
  • The mobility agreement must detail subjects, teaching content, days and hours.
A valid Erasmus+ inter-institutional agreement is mandatory for teaching mobility.

Application and Documents

Applications are submitted exclusively via TurnaPortal using e-Government authentication. All uploaded documents must be in PDF format.

Required documents

  1. An invitation letter from the receiving HEI explicitly confirming acceptance under Erasmus+.
  2. A Staff Mobility Agreement for Teaching signed/approved by the staff member and receiving institution, with University approval finalised before mobility.
  3. A valid inter-institutional agreement and a teaching schedule meeting the minimum hours.
  4. Language proficiency evidence and documentation for any bonus points claimed.
Supporting documents are required for the relevant bonus points. Individual application deadlines are stated in the call, not in this programme guide.

Language evidence and tie-break

  • The 2026 call lists ÜDS, KPDS, YDS, TOEFL, PTE, YÖKDİL and IELTS with ÖSYM equivalence. No expiry requirement is stated for language evidence.
  • A degree entirely taught in the activity language may be scored 90/100; at least one year in an institution teaching in that language may be scored 70/100; at least one year teaching in the language may be scored 90/100.
  • For equal scores, priority goes first to first-time participants, then to longer service at Fenerbahçe University.
These are the 2026 KA131 first-call criteria, not permanent rules for all future calls.

Mobility Process

  1. Obtain the invitation and agree on a teaching schedule with the host.
  2. Submit the application and all supporting PDF documents through TurnaPortal.
  3. Follow preliminary results and the appeal period in the individual call.
  4. After final selection, obtain required approvals and sign the grant agreement; submit withdrawal by the stated deadline if not participating.
  5. Complete the mobility, submit the certificate of attendance and travel evidence, and complete the Participant Report in the Beneficiary Module.
