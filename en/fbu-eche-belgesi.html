Erasmus Charter for Higher Education (ECHE)

The Erasmus Charter for Higher Education (ECHE) sets out the fundamental principles and quality requirements that higher education institutions commit to when participating in the Erasmus+ Programme for 2021–2027. Fenerbahçe University holds a charter awarded by the European Commission.

The ECHE covers fair and transparent selection procedures, equal opportunities and inclusion, recognition of academic achievements abroad, support for participants before, during and after mobility, and practices relating to digitalisation and environmental sustainability.
