Peer Mentoring Programme

What Is the Peer Mentoring Programme?

The Peer Mentoring Programme is a volunteer-based support network designed to help newly enrolled students adjust to Istanbul and to life at our university.

Students can participate in the programme in two roles:

  • Mentor: An experienced student familiar with the city and campus life who helps newcomers settle in and build social connections.
  • Mentee: A newly enrolled student who wishes to become familiar with university life and the city.

The friendships formed through this voluntary programme help students discover our cultural diversity and enrich their university experience.

Duties and Responsibilities of Peer Mentors

  • Organise regular meetings to provide information about the university and its academic procedures.
  • Provide basic information about university regulations and guidelines.
  • Guide students through course registration and academic matters.
  • Offer practical support with everyday life while students adjust to the university and Istanbul.
  • Encourage active participation in campus and city social and cultural life.
  • Introduce students to student clubs and sporting, cultural and artistic activities.
  • Help students resolve difficulties and refer them to the relevant administrative units when necessary.
  • Conduct all mentoring activities with respect for confidentiality and trust.

Eligibility to Become a Mentor

Students wishing to become peer mentors are expected to meet the following criteria:

  • Have completed at least one (1) full academic year in a faculty, excluding the preparatory year.
  • Have a minimum cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of 2.50 .
  • Have no disciplinary sanctions during their studies.

Applications and Contact: To become a mentor or mentee, please contact us at erasmus@fbu.edu.tr .
