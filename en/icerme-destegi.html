Erasmus+ Inclusion Support

What Is Inclusion Support?

The Erasmus+ Programme encourages the participation of individuals with fewer opportunities. Potential participants who would be unable to take part in a project or mobility activity without additional financial support due to their physical, mental or health circumstances may be eligible for Inclusion Support.

Application Process and Required Documents

Once a participant eligible for inclusion support has been selected, any additional costs are estimated and a request for supplementary funding is submitted through the International Office.

  • Application Deadline: Requests for additional funding must be submitted during the project agreement period and, in all cases, no later than 60 days before the agreement end date . Funding increases cannot be requested after the activity has ended.
  • Supporting Documents: The application must explain why inclusion support is needed and include supporting evidence (e.g. a disability certificate or a medical report specifying the disability and its degree, issued within the last three months ; or an up-to-date medical report for a chronic condition).
  • Host Institution Commitment: The application must include a statement confirming that the receiving higher education institution is aware of the participant’s support needs and has appropriate facilities.

Assessment and Grant Payment

The requested additional funding and the reasons for the request must be explained in detail in the form. The amount requested must be directly related to enabling the participant to take part in the activity.

  • Applications for additional funding are assessed individually by the Turkish National Agency , which determines the appropriate grant amount.
  • The final amount of inclusion support is based on actual eligible expenditure (supported by invoices).
  • At the end of the activity, invoices documenting the use of the additional grant must be collected and retained in the participant’s file for possible audits.

Important Note: Expenses without supporting documents or invoices, and expenses exceeding the inclusion support grant specified in the agreement, are not eligible for reimbursement even if they have been incurred.
