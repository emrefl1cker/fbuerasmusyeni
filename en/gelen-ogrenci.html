Nomination & Application

Who can come to FBU as an Erasmus+ student?

For incoming Erasmus+ mobility for studies, your home university must have a valid Inter-Institutional Agreement (IIA) with Fenerbahçe University. Contact your home university’s Erasmus+ coordinator before arranging a nomination.

How does nomination work?

Your home university’s coordinator should email erasmus@fbu.edu.tr before the nomination deadline with the following information:

  • Erasmus+ code of the sending university
  • Student’s full name and email address
  • Department and subject area code
  • Study cycle
  • Planned duration and semester of mobility

What happens next?

Once your nomination has been received, our International Relations Department will send you the application instructions.

Arrival, Registration & Orientation

When you arrive at Fenerbahçe University, please contact the International Relations Department. The team will guide you through the initial registration steps and direct you to your Erasmus+ departmental coordinator and the Student Affairs Office for course registration.

  • Complete the necessary university registration procedures.
  • Provide one biometric photograph to the Student Affairs Office for your student ID card, when requested.
  • Meet your Erasmus+ departmental coordinator to discuss your courses and Learning Agreement.
  • Attend the orientation activities announced for incoming exchange students.
Need help? Please contact erasmus@fbu.edu.tr before or after your arrival.

Health Insurance

Incoming Erasmus+ students should arrange health insurance valid in Türkiye for their mobility period and check that the cover meets the requirements applicable to them.

Depending on eligibility and individual circumstances, possible arrangements include:

  • General Health Insurance through Türkiye’s Social Security Institution (SGK)
  • Applicable bilateral social security agreements
  • Private health insurance from the home country that is valid in Türkiye
  • Private health insurance issued in Türkiye

Please check the scope of coverage and relevant requirements before arrival. The International Relations Department can guide you to the relevant offices.

Residence Permit

Incoming exchange students are requested to contact the International Relations Department upon arrival at Fenerbahçe University for information and guidance regarding residence permit procedures. The department will provide guidance during the application process.
