Who Can Apply for Traineeship Mobility?

Erasmus+ traineeship mobility enables students to undertake work-based training abroad at a business, research institute, laboratory or other organisation related to their academic discipline. The traineeship must complement their study programme; attending courses at a higher education institution is not a traineeship.

Academic status and grades

  • Be a full-time student enrolled in an associate, bachelor's or postgraduate programme.
  • Minimum cumulative GPA: 2.20/4.00 for associate/bachelor's students; 2.50/4.00 for postgraduate students.
  • Under the examined call, students transferring from an associate programme without a current transcript may use an associate graduation GPA of at least 2.20/4.00; preparatory and first-year students without an established GPA must have a high-school graduation grade of at least 75/100.
  • New postgraduate students without a GPA in the current programme may apply using a documented previous bachelor's or master's graduation GPA.

Other eligibility rules

  • Double-major students may apply through only one major in a single application period.
  • Students may apply while their registration is suspended, but may not undertake mobility during their leave of absence.
  • Thesis master's/doctoral students who have not completed their course obligations require the appropriate favourable academic opinions and a Graduate School board decision.
  • Studies and traineeships undertaken in the same study cycle, funded or unfunded, generally count towards a combined 12-month limit. Certain integrated/single-cycle programmes may allow 24 months.
  • The examined call excludes mobility during certain final semesters associated with doctoral qualification, thesis proposal defence or maximum programme duration. Affected students should consult their Graduate School and International Office.
  • For recent-graduate traineeships, the application must be submitted before graduation and the activity must be completed within 12 months of graduation.

Duration and host organisation

  • The examined call requires a traineeship of at least 60 days, with funding for a maximum of 90 days. The activity period and funded period need not be identical.
  • The student is responsible for securing a host organisation. Under the relevant programme conditions, an inter-institutional agreement is not required for a traineeship.
Project-specific deadlines, acceptance-letter submission dates, grants and academic requirements may differ in later calls. Check the individual call page.

How to Apply and Required Documents

This guide follows the 2026 KA131 traineeship call. Final application deadlines, examination dates and acceptance-letter submission deadlines are published on the relevant call page.

  1. Read the call. Check its project, academic year, application window, funded quota, language examination and assessment timetable in Announcements.
  2. Apply on TurnaPortal. Use e-Government identification via turnaportal.ua.gov.tr.
  3. Upload a current, verifiable QR-coded transcript. The examined call requires a transcript issued within the application window and uploaded as a PDF. Newly enrolled postgraduate students may need to upload transcripts from their previous studies as well.
  4. Upload an acceptance letter if available. Under the examined call, an acceptance letter submitted at the time of application earns +10 points. If you do not yet have one, check the separate acceptance-letter deadline and conditions of the call.
  5. Follow examination and assessment notices. Eligibility for the language exam, preliminary results, appeals and final results are published on the same call detail page.

Required content of an acceptance letter

  • An explicit reference to Erasmus+ Student Mobility for Traineeships.
  • Planned start and end dates (allow for visa processing).
  • Traineeship position or topic.
  • Host organisation's details.
  • Name, position and contact details of the responsible signatory.
The Turkish and English source calls cite different academic years for the prior transcript of newly enrolled postgraduate students without an established GPA. Confirm the required transcript period in the current call or with the office before submitting.

What counts as a Digital Opportunity Traineeship?

The source call lists software/web development, data analytics and visualisation, cybersecurity, digital marketing, design and artificial intelligence as examples. Routine data entry, general customer service and ordinary office duties do not qualify as DOTs.

Traineeship Mobility: Step-by-Step

  1. Find a suitable host. The traineeship should be relevant to your field. An inter-institutional agreement is not a prerequisite for the traineeship.
  2. Apply during the call. Upload required documents and, if available, an acceptance letter via TurnaPortal.
  3. Attend the exam and assessment stages. Follow published exam lists, preliminary results and appeals on the individual call page.
  4. Confirm the acceptance letter. Agree on the activities, host contact, dates and job description with the host organisation.
  5. Complete mobility documents. Prepare the Learning Agreement for Traineeships and the visa, insurance, grant agreement and other documents required by the International Office.
  6. Undertake your traineeship. Notify the office of any changes to dates, activities or documents as required.
  7. Complete post-mobility formalities. Obtain the host's completion/evaluation documents and submit the required closing documents for academic recognition or recording.

Selection by Fenerbahçe University does not itself constitute acceptance by a traineeship host. Host approval, immigration and insurance requirements must also be met.

How to Find a Traineeship Host

In traineeship mobility, an inter-institutional agreement is not required as a general prerequisite; finding a suitable host organisation is the student's responsibility. Always check the relevant call's eligibility and deadlines.

  1. Define your goals. Identify tasks related to your department, desired skills and preferred countries/cities.
  2. Use academic contacts. Speak with instructors, supervisors and professionals in your subject area.
  3. Research organisations. Explore companies, laboratories, traineeship portals and professional networks.
  4. Send tailored applications. Include your CV, a concise motivation, possible dates and a short explanation of Erasmus+ traineeship mobility.
  5. Check the acceptance letter. Make sure it includes complete host details, the responsible person, position and dates.

Digital Opportunity Traineeships (DOTs)

The cited 2026 call lists data analytics, software and web development, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, digital marketing and design among its examples. Routine data entry and general office duties are excluded from the DOTs category.

Before accepting an offer, check the job description, visa/accommodation arrangements, insurance requirements and the identity of the host organisation.
