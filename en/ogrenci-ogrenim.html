Who Can Apply for Mobility for Studies?

Erasmus+ Student Mobility for Studies allows eligible students to complete part of their programme at a partner higher education institution abroad. This guide is based on the general provisions in the supplied 2026 KA131 call documents. The specific conditions and subsequent official corrections of each call take precedence.

Associate and undergraduate students

  • Be enrolled full-time at Fenerbahçe University and have a cumulative GPA of at least 2.20/4.00.
  • Eligibility where a student has not yet received a university GPA, and the use of previous graduation grades, depend on the relevant call. Check the call-specific provisions regarding preparatory-year and first-year students.
  • Double-major students may apply under only one major during the same application period.
  • If courses are taught in a language other than English, provide evidence of the language proficiency required by the host institution.

Graduate students

  • Be enrolled full-time and have a GPA of at least 2.50/4.00. Where permitted by the call, applicants without a GPA in their current programme may apply with a documented GPA from a previous degree.
  • Research assistants must obtain written approval from the relevant unit head at the time of application.
  • Thesis-track master's and doctoral students who have not completed their coursework require the positive opinions of their supervisor and head of department, together with the Graduate School Administrative Board's decision.

Additional conditions for all students

  • Students on leave may apply, but may not undertake mobility during the period in which their enrolment is suspended.
  • Across study and traineeship mobilities, the total duration in the same study cycle may not exceed 12 months, whether funded or zero-grant. The limit may be 24 months for certain integrated or single-cycle programmes.
  • Applications for studies must relate to a valid inter-institutional agreement in the student's own department/programme. The host institution's academic, admission and language requirements must also be met.
If this guide and a specific call appear to differ, request written clarification from the International Office. Official call-specific conditions and published amendments are decisive.

How to Apply and Required Documents

These steps are drawn from the supplied 2026 KA131 studies call. Application dates, exam arrangements, and call-specific document requirements appear on the relevant announcement page.

  1. Review the current call. Check the academic year, semester, opening/closing dates, funded places and any preferences restricted in that call.
  2. Research partner universities. Check the agreements for your department, course catalogue, teaching language, nomination and host application deadlines.
  3. Apply through TurnaPortal. Use e-Government authentication at turnaportal.ua.gov.tr.
  4. Upload a verifiable transcript. The supplied call requires a QR-coded/verifiable transcript from OIS or e-Government. OIS preview transcripts are not accepted, and uploaded documents must be in PDF format.
  5. Check your preferences. The reviewed 2026 call asks applicants to use all three choices; confirm the allowed number in each future call.
  6. Follow the evaluation timeline. Language exam lists, preliminary results, appeals and final decisions appear on the same announcement's detail page.

Important reminders

  • Internal placement does not guarantee admission by the receiving institution.
  • Host institutions can revise their entry conditions or course availability; applicants must monitor these updates.
  • A mobility place awarded for one academic year cannot automatically be transferred to a different academic year.
  • Application and document submission schedules vary by call.

Student Mobility for Studies: Step-by-Step Guide

Students must fulfil their responsibilities on time at each stage of mobility. The steps are explained below.

1. Nomination and Application to the Receiving Institution

The Erasmus Office nominates the student to the receiving institution by email. The student must then prepare the required documents and submit their own application before the receiving institution’s deadline.

2. Acceptance Letter and Visa Process

If the application is accepted, the receiving institution issues an Acceptance Letter. Applying for a visa before receiving this letter is not recommended. Visa, travel and accommodation arrangements are entirely the student’s responsibility.

3. Before the Mobility

  • Students must prepare their Online Learning Agreement (OLA) via the EWP/OLA platform.
  • Students must take between 24 and 36 ECTS credits per semester (preferably 30 ECTS). The difference in credits between equivalent FBU courses may not exceed ±3 ECTS.
  • At least 15 days before mobility begins , the student must submit the Acceptance Letter, OLA, TEB EUR bank account details, Faculty Board Decision and health insurance documents to the International Office and sign the Grant Agreement .

4. During the Mobility

If course changes are necessary at the receiving institution, the changes must be recorded in the OLA within 4–7 weeks of the start of the academic semester , and the Faculty Board Decision must be updated.
Important: Students must successfully complete at least 21 ECTS of courses at the receiving institution in one semester. Otherwise, 20% will be deducted from their final grant.

5. After the Mobility and Credit Recognition

Upon return, students must contact their departmental coordinator with the original Transcript of Records to complete credit recognition. They must then submit the Certificate of Attendance, the host institution’s Transcript of Records and an updated FBU transcript to the International Office. Finally, students must complete the online EU Survey.
